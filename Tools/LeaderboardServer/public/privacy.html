What we collect

  • Player name you choose. It's shown publicly on leaderboards. Please don't use your real full name.
  • Social media handle (optional). It's never shown publicly and is used only to contact contest winners.
  • Optional 4-digit PIN, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so you can use your name on another device.
  • Game results: run times, waves, enemies defeated, coins and similar scores.
  • Gameplay analytics: how the game is played, such as controls used, menu choices, deaths and session length. These are tied to a random ID, not to your identity.
  • Technical data needed to run the service and stop cheating, such as a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address.

We don't ask for your email, real name, address or payment details in the game.

How we use it

  • To run the leaderboards and weekly contests, and to contact winners.
  • To find and remove cheating.
  • To improve the game's controls, difficulty and design.
