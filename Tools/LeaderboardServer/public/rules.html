4. Contest weeks

Each contest week starts Monday at 12:00 AM Pacific Time and ends Sunday at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. The Sponsor's server clock is the official timekeeper.

5. Categories and how winners are decided

This is a game of skill. One winner is selected in each category based solely on the leaderboard at the end of the contest week:

  • Story: Fastest Run: the shortest time to complete Story Mode, starting from Chapter 1.
  • Infinite: Most Waves: the highest wave reached in Infinite Mode.
  • Infinite: Most Washes: the most enemies defeated in a single Infinite Mode run.
  • Infinite: Longest Run: the longest single Infinite Mode run.

Ties go to the player who set the score first. A player may win in more than one category [or: one prize per player per week].

If a winner is disqualified, the next eligible player in that category wins. Offensive player names may be renamed or removed.
